## 5. Continuity and operational assumptions

- Trusted OS/SDK/PostgreSQL clocks must remain continuously within the accepted bound DURING comparison. Pre/post samples cannot detect all hidden excursions. Durable DB wall watermark is not a physical monotonic clock; whole DB rollback can erase it.
- Replay key/history, retired generations/SIDs, governance and audit are trusted persistent history, not authenticated against a database-owner rollback. A snapshot may restore old key, spent-code absence, revoked sessions and watermark together. SQL cannot independently detect that arbitrary restoration. A disposable rollback demonstration is evidence of a LIMIT, never safe restore certification.
- Provider secret change may invalidate cookies and make encrypted factors/backups unreadable. No automated keyring/rotation/migration is approved here; do not destroy old decryptability or remove the last Owner factor to make a test pass.
- Worker orphan cleanup is bounded deny-only maintenance, not automatic recovery or health. Source+worker transactions are not distributed atomic. Backlog/audit failure can intentionally stop otherwise valid provider writes and logins.
- Same-process AUTH/STAFF/VERIFIER custody is not process-compromise isolation. TOTP remains phishable. Common loopback budgets/global governance gate constrain availability; no load/DDoS certification.
